# Product Overview

**GlowHomeo** is a modern, production-grade telemedicine and patient engagement platform built specifically for homeopathic clinics. 

## Vision
To seamlessly bridge the gap between doctor visits by combining a powerful in-clinic CRM/EMR with an automated, WhatsApp-first patient engagement ecosystem.

## Core Pillars

1. **Doctor Portal (Web):** A high-performance Next.js application that handles the 9-step consultation workflow, prescription generation, video telemedicine, and a unified patient inbox.
2. **WhatsApp Patient Experience:** Patients interact with the clinic entirely through WhatsApp. Two dedicated WABA numbers handle proactive automated care (reminders, check-ins, LMS) and free-form clinical messaging.
3. **Care Plan Engine:** Automated distribution of diet plans, lifestyle tips, educational videos, and medication reminders directly to the patient's phone.
4. **Data Privacy & Security:** Multi-tenant architecture secured by Supabase Row Level Security (RLS) ensuring strict isolation between clinic data.
