# GlowHomeo Assist — Clinical Workflow Overview

*A presentation guide for experienced homeopaths*

---

## 1. Our Philosophy

Every good consultation walks a fine line. On one side sits the need for a faithful, precise record: the kind of case material that does justice to the *whole* person, not a fragment. On the other side sits the living moment — the unhurried listening, the trust, the small cues that are easy to miss when your attention is pulled away.

GlowHomeo Assist is built for that balance. The challenge we acknowledge is not “more typing” or “more technology,” but the tension between **staying with the person in the room** and **honouring what you heard in a way you can return to, share, and build on**. We believe thoughtful structure should arrive *after* the conversation, not in place of it — so the heart of the work remains where it has always lived: in your attention, your judgment, and the therapeutic relationship you hold for each patient.

---

## 2. The Clinical Rhythm — A Nine-Step Journey

The workflow is organized into **three natural phases** that follow how many homeopaths already think about a case, from first contact to long-term continuity.

### Phase 1 — The Consultation: Arrival to Case-Taking

*From the first hello to a settled picture of the case as it is today.*

| Step | What it supports |
|------|------------------|
| **1. Preparing the ground** | You start from a light sense of *who* is in front of you before you step into the room — the continuity of their story, not a cold list. |
| **2. The conversation, not a questionnaire** | The encounter stays conversational. The person speaks in their own order; the platform is not a wall of fields demanding answers mid-sentence. |
| **3. A gentle mirror for what was said** | As the interview unfolds, support is available to shape what you heard into a clearer thread — rephrased, organized, always visible for you to correct, never replacing your ear. |

Together, these steps protect **natural case-taking** while still helping you and your patient move from a loose narrative toward something you can work with, without the feeling of “filling a form at the same time as listening.”

### Phase 2 — The Treatment: Notes & Prescription

*Where your clinical mind turns understanding into a durable record and a plan.*

| Step | What it supports |
|------|------------------|
| **4. A pause to reflect** | You step back from the flow of the visit and look at the material as a whole before anything is “locked in.” |
| **5. The written case, under your signature** | Observations, emphasis, and nuance are yours to **finalize**. Nothing in this picture is published to the case until **you** say it reflects your understanding. |
| **6. Remedies in the language of your practice** | You map what you have chosen to the way your clinic documents remedies — in terms your patient can recognize where appropriate, and in terms your professional judgment requires for clarity. |

In this phase, the platform is a **scribe and organizer of your own conclusions**, not a co-author of the prescription.

### Phase 3 — The Healing Journey: Follow-up & Continuity

*The months and years in which a remedy proves itself and the case deepens.*

| Step | What it supports |
|------|------------------|
| **7. Remembering the last chapter** | Each return begins with a sense of the last visit, so you are not rebuilding context from memory alone. |
| **8. The long story, kept in view** | The thread of the case — chief complaints, emotional tone, concomitants, what changed, what did not — stays visible in a way that respects the **timeline** of the *whole* person, not a single visit in isolation. |
| **9. A steady hand for what comes next** | Follow-up rhythm, reminders, and the connective tissue between visits help you and your patient **stay in step** with the case as it actually evolves, so the next prescription is not written in a void. |

This is where GlowHomeo Assist leans into **continuity of care** — the sense that the case file breathes with the person, from first interview through many seasons of change.

## 3. The “Doctor in Control” Promise

GlowHomeo Assist is offered as a way to **organize thought, time, and paper** — not to displace the clinical act.

- **Judgment and responsibility** for the remedy, the potency, the repetition, and the explanation to the patient remain **entirely** with the physician.
- The system may suggest, tidy, or remind, but it does **not** make therapeutic decisions, rank remedies, or replace the careful, intuitive, and repertory-supported work you were trained to do.
- The written record, when shared or printed, is what **you** have agreed reflects your understanding, so your name continues to stand on solid ground, ethically and professionally.

In short: **your eye, your ear, and your hand on the case — always.**

## 4. The Feedback Loop — Three Questions for You

To learn whether this rhythm truly fits **your** room and **your** style of homeopathic practice, we would be grateful for your candid reflection. These questions are offered in a spirit of partnership, not examination:

1. **In your current clinic, which of these nine steps — or the transition between them — most often becomes the *bottleneck* or the *source of stress*?**  
2. **Does this workflow, as a whole, feel like something that *supports* the way you consult, or like something that could *get in the way*? Where is the line for you?**  
3. **What is *one* thing you still do by hand, on paper, or in scattered notes, that you wish a trusted system could *carry* for you — so that more of you could stay with the patient, visit after visit?**
